# Experiment record

- Dataset: 20 original hand-written examples in the reusable fabric bags and small organisers category.
- Model: decoder-only GPT, 2 transformer blocks, 4 attention heads, 64-dimensional embeddings, 356-token BPE vocabulary.
- Training: 400 optimizer steps, seed 42, CPU, next-token cross-entropy objective.
- Training loss: 5.89045 at step 1; 0.32493 at step 400.
- Generation sample: see `sample_generation.txt` (generated by the trained checkpoint with the CLI).

## Evaluation interpretation and limits

This is a training demonstration, not a production-quality language model. The falling training loss shows that the network learned patterns in this tiny corpus, but it does not establish generalisation. The generated sample may contain malformed or repetitive text. The corpus is too small for a statistically meaningful held-out quality score; no accuracy or human-preference score is claimed. A next evaluation step is to collect a larger permissioned corpus, reserve a held-out test set, and have reviewers score factuality, readability and relevance against a simple nearest-example baseline.
